ABOUT

Built for storytellers, creators, and future leaders.

ELJCMASHS is a learner-centered school that blends media arts, communication, and creativity into a dynamic learning experience guided by academic excellence and community purpose.

Media Arts Communication Leadership Student Voice

School identity

Creative. Critical. Community-driven.

Students are encouraged to think deeply, create boldly, and lead responsibly in a campus culture shaped by collaboration and artistic expression.

VISION & MISSION

Learning grounded in purpose and excellence.

DepEd Vision

Filipino learners becoming globally competitive and responsible citizens.

DepEd envisions a future where every learner is equipped with the values, skills, and knowledge needed to contribute to nation-building and participate meaningfully in a changing world.

DepEd Mission

To protect and promote the right of every learner to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education.

DepEd is committed to delivering a responsive, accessible, and learner-centered education that nurtures critical thinking, creativity, and social responsibility.

School Mission

To develop media-literate, confident, and socially responsible students.

ELJCMASHS helps students grow through strong academics, practical media skills, leadership opportunities, and a campus culture that values both craftsmanship and character.

SCHOOL HISTORY

ELJCMASHS through the years.

2013

Foundations in policy

The K-12 Law created the policy environment for a specialized public school focused on media arts, communication, and creative industries.

Sources: First Balfour, ELJ school page

2016

The soft launch

As construction and preparations advanced, the school began taking shape as a media arts institution with a clear identity rooted in learning, creativity, and student voice.

Sources: First Balfour, Quezon City government, ELJ official page

2018

Full independence

ELJCMASHS became an independent senior high school committed to media arts excellence, student leadership, and creative learning experiences aligned with national education goals.

Sources: Quezon City government, ELJ Facebook page, DepEd

Today

A growing creative community

The school now continues to grow as a vibrant campus for media, arts, communication, and student leadership—nurturing a generation of learners ready to create, inform, and inspire.

Sources: DepEd, ELJ official page, Quezon City
